About Alaska Energy Network

Powering the future of digital infrastructure through a nationwide network of participants contributing computing resources and earning monthly rewards.

Who We Are

Alaska Energy Network is a distributed computing platform that connects participants across the United States to a modern digital infrastructure ecosystem. Our network enables individuals to contribute computing resources that support advanced technologies while earning rewards based on verified participation.

By combining innovative infrastructure management, transparent performance tracking, and a nationwide participation model, we create opportunities for individuals to engage with the growing digital economy while supporting critical computing operations.

Our Mission

To build a transparent and accessible distributed energy network that allows individuals to participate in powering the future of digital infrastructure while receiving fair monthly rewards.

Our Vision

To become the leading distributed computing and energy participation network in the United States, creating value for both participants and emerging technologies.

Our Values

Transparency • Innovation • Security • Reliability • Community
